02The Story

A hazard on the forest floor, refined into something you keep.

Every summer, the Chir pines of Himachal shed millions of tonnes of dry needles. Rich in resin and slow to decompose, they blanket the forest floor and become kindling — the single largest cause of the wildfires that ravage the Himalayan foothills each year.

Abhinav Talwar grew up watching those fires. He understood that the only way to stop them was to clear the floor of its fuel — and that the only way to do that, year after year, was to give the needles a worth of their own.

So we collect them. By hand. Together with the women's collectives — the Mahila Mandals — of the Kangra valley, we walk the forests before the fire season and gather what the trees let fall. Then, through a patented process bound with limestone and clay, we press those needles into objects that are quiet, durable and entirely free of plastic.

Nothing is cut. Nothing is mined for oil. A problem is lifted from the mountain, and a vessel is put in your hand.

Abhinav TalwarFounder · Climatepreneur

04From needle to object

The hands behind every piece.

A short supply chain with a long view — four steps between the mountain and your table.

01

Gathered

Before fire season, Mahila Mandals hand-pick fallen needles from the forest floor — clearing the mountain as they work.

02

Bound

Needles are combined with natural limestone and clay — never resin glues — and prepared for moulding.

03

Pressed

A patented injection process forms each vessel, extinguishing flammability while keeping natural antimicrobial qualities.

04

Kept

Lightweight, unbreakable, microwave-safe — made to be used daily and to return to the earth when finally done.

06The Material

One ingredient, honestly named.

Chir pine needles, limestone and clay. That is the whole recipe. No petrochemical binders, no greenwashed mystery compounds — only what the mountain already provides.

01

Unbreakable

Built for the daily drop, the careless tap, the child's hand. No more ceramic shards.

02

Microwave safe

Reheat without a second thought — the composite holds up where plastic warps.

03

Naturally antimicrobial

The pine's own defences survive the process, keeping surfaces quietly clean.

Biodegradable

When a vessel finally retires, it returns to soil — not to a landfill for centuries.
